About

I'm Chekus Joseph. I build backend systems and web apps in Go and Python — usually Flask on the server side — and I do the front-end work myself too: Tailwind, vanilla JS, sometimes Flutter for mobile.

Most of what's on this site started as something I needed to solve myself: a portfolio that didn't repeat the same markup across six pages, a calculator that runs in the browser via WebAssembly, a task tracker I actually use. I like understanding a system down to the terminal — configuring Linux, chasing a broken PATH variable, working out why a service won't start — because that's usually where the real problems live, not in the UI.

I won't claim years of enterprise experience I don't have. What I can say: I finish what I start, I test what I ship, and if something's broken I'd rather dig into the actual cause than cover it up. If that's the kind of developer you're looking for, get in touch.
