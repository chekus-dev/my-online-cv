about

Self-taught, full-stack, and shipping.

I'm Chekus Joseph, a full-stack, AI-native engineer working mainly in Go and Python. I build complete web products on my own: backends in Go and Flask, PostgreSQL databases, Tailwind interfaces, and deployment. I'm self-taught, and I learned by building real things and debugging them until they worked.

That habit shows in the work. I started with Python and Flask, then picked up Go to understand servers closer to the metal. When my local MySQL install broke while building my budget tracker, I traced it through systemd and down to a kernel-level AppArmor denial instead of working around it. I then moved the app to hosted PostgreSQL and rewrote the MySQL-specific SQL to match.

My strongest project is Budget Tracker, a live Go and PostgreSQL app with email password reset, per-user and per-IP brute-force limits, server-verified reCAPTCHA, soft delete with undo, embedded migrations, and unit tests. Alongside it are a Flask task manager, this portfolio site, and a series of standard-library Go HTTP servers that cover routing, JSON, readiness checks, timeouts, and graceful shutdown.

I build with AI in the loop: LLM tools help me plan, scaffold, debug, and review, and I read, test, and own every line that ships. I work in small, tested steps, document the setbacks as well as the wins, and keep each layer working before adding the next. My longer-term goal is to build my own products and, eventually, a company.

Across my two GitHub accounts I also build smaller tools, including a Go text processor and ASCII-art web apps, and I've explored mobile with Kotlin and Flutter. My repositories include original projects and clearly identified forks, such as a Go artist-and-concert tracker where I contributed as a partner. I link upstream projects on the portfolio so the distinction is visible.
