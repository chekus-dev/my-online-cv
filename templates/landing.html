about

I own what I build, end to end.

I started with Python and Flask, then picked up Go because I wanted to understand what happens closer to the metal. That curiosity carried into Linux system administration. I’m comfortable in a terminal, configuring servers, and diagnosing things most developers treat as someone else’s problem.

I work well independently, communicate clearly in writing, and push code that’s meant to be read by the next person, not just run by a machine. If you need someone who can take a ticket from spec to shipped without hand-holding, I can do that. My longer-term goal is to build my own products and, eventually, a company.
